視覺設計規則

  • 每張照片獨立輸出，不拼接多張照片。
  • 固定 3:4 直式構圖，上下兩區各佔 50%。
  • 上半部保留真實照片的結構、材質、光影與色彩，只做輕度高級攝影調色。
  • 下半部抽取最具辨識度的主體、輪廓、姿勢與敘事關係，改寫成極簡等距插畫或微縮模型。
  • 以紙張、微型底座、留白、體積對比、投影與少量細節建立尺度感與孤寂感。
  • 色彩從原照片提取，整理成低雜訊、柔和、克制的有限色盤。
  • 從地點、主題、動作或情緒抽取短英文標題，融入留白與版面節奏。
  • 避免卡通感、高飽和衝突、無關的固定配色、塑膠 3D 感、場景堆疊與電商模板感。

Please make each photo I uploaded into an independent high-end design poster, do not stitch more pictures, and output each photo separately. The whole adopts a 3:4 vertical composition, and the height of the upper and lower two areas is strictly 1:1, each accounting for 50% of the picture.

The first half retains the original photo, maintaining the main structure, real texture, natural light and shadow and original color atmosphere, and only slightly advanced photography color adjustment, so that it has the texture of art magazines, independent publications and exhibition photography. In order to adapt the frame, the sky, ground or environmental background can be naturally expanded, but the subject should not be stretched, distorted or changed.

In the second half, the most recognizable subject, outline, posture and narrative relationship in the photo are extracted and reconstructed into exquisite minimalist equidistant perspective illustrations, such as a miniature three-dimensional model or architectural sandbox displayed on paper. Do not mechanically reproduce the details of the photo, but re-express the subject by generalizing the body block, clear edges, concise hierarchy and micro-scene relationships, so that it can retain the identity characteristics that can be identified at a glance, and at the same time have a quiet, restrained and poetic editing illustration temperament.

The composition logic of equidistant perspective, miniature landscape, and paper model is adopted as a whole: the main image is cut and placed on a small piece of paper or micro-base, leaving a large area of white space around it, and a sense of scale and loneliness is established through volume comparison, edge cutting, light projection and a small number of details. A very small number of auxiliary elements can be added to support the main body, but it must not form a second visual center.

The color matching is completely extracted from the original photo above, giving priority to retaining the most recognizable, temperamental and most representative colors of the subject, and then the complex colors are summarized, purified, softened and unified into a limited color plate. Retain the cold and warm relationship and color character of the original picture, but reduce the variegation and visual noise, so as to make the color calmer, restrained, softer, and have a sense of editing illustration on paper; distinguish blocks through the same color color depth, adjacent color relationship and a small amount of color difference, and do not add a fixed color system that is not related to the original picture out of thin air. Use fine ink lines, flat color surfaces, slight shadows and paper particles to express the structure and layer.

The text is cleverly involved as part of the composition, extracting a short English title from the theme, place, action, state or emotion of the photo, with a very small number of numbers, short sentences or micro-annotations. The text maintains a thin, restrained and modern sense of editing. It can be arranged along the white edge, the main base, the horizontal axis or the equidistant structure to form a quiet and exquisite layout rhythm with the graphics.

The overall reference minimalist equidistant illustration, miniature three-dimensional model, paper landscape and advanced editing design, presenting a quiet, poetic, restrained, exquisite and collectible visual temperament. Avoid cartoon feeling, high saturation color clash, fixed color matching unrelated to the original picture, plastic 3D feeling, complex scene accumulation, e-commerce feeling and template feeling.

實作拆解與使用建議

  • **適合素材**：建築、古鎮、海岸線、橋梁、寺院、街景與有清楚主體的旅行照片。
  • **先做單張測試**：一次上傳多張時，模型可能偷做拼貼；可先用一張照片確認上下分區與主體保真度。
  • **為地標提供文字**：若景點辨識度不高，可在 prompt 前補上 `[place]`、地點名稱或文化背景，降低模型把地標泛化成普通建築的機率。
  • **文字後製**：生成模型常會產生錯字或不穩定字體；英文標題建議保留短字串，必要時在 Figma、Canva 或 Photoshop 後製。
  • **避免主體變形**：上半部強調不得拉伸、扭曲或改變主體；若是人物或複雜建築，仍應逐張檢查臉部、招牌、窗戶與結構線。
  • **不要把「微縮模型」誤當 3D 建模**：這是影像生成的視覺效果，並非可直接用於製造或測量的 CAD／3D 資產。
