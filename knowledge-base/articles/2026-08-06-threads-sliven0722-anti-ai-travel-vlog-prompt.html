Threads｜反 AI 感旅行 Vlog：讓影片像朋友跟拍，而不是觀光廣告的提示詞

為什麼保存

現在 AI 影片最容易露餡的地方，往往不是畫質不夠好，而是畫面太完美：鏡頭過度穩定、人物每一秒都像在擺拍、轉場精準得像廣告，反而讓人一眼就知道這是 AI 生成。

這組提示詞的價值，在於它反過來要求模型保留人類拍攝時常見的小瑕疵：朋友跟拍的輕微晃動、臨時重新構圖、自然曝光變化、人物沒有照劇本演出的真實反應。最後效果更像是一位朋友拿著手機，陪主角從早上一路拍到海邊日落的旅行 Vlog，而不是品牌整組團隊拍攝的觀光廣告。

核心觀念：真實感來自不完美

這組提示詞真正重要的不是「旅行」本身，而是建立一套反 AI 感的拍攝規則：

  • 不要一直要求 `cinematic`、`perfect`、`epic`。
  • 鏡頭可以稍微跟不上人物。
  • 構圖可以偶爾被路人、物件或空間限制擋住。
  • 人物不必每秒都看鏡頭。
  • 可以保留自動對焦、focus breathing、曝光調整與手持晃動的痕跡。
  • 人物反應要像真的在旅行，而不是在完成鏡頭指令。

換句話說，真實感通常不是來自更多電影術語，而是來自那些人類拍攝時無法完全控制的小瑕疵。

可重用提示詞

```text

Create a 30-second, highly realistic personal travel vlog filmed by a friend following the main subject throughout one complete day.

REFERENCE SUBJECT

Use the person shown in the uploaded reference image as the only main character.

Preserve the subject’s exact identity throughout every shot, including:

  • facial structure
  • eyes, nose, lips, and skin tone
  • hairstyle and hair length
  • age and body proportions
  • outfit continuity unless an outfit change is explicitly described

The subject must remain recognizably the same person from beginning to end.

CAMERA LANGUAGE

The footage should feel like it was casually recorded by a real friend using a smartphone or compact mirrorless camera.

Use:

  • natural handheld camera movement
  • subtle human camera shake
  • occasional imperfect framing
  • spontaneous reframing while walking
  • realistic autofocus adjustments
  • mild focus breathing
  • natural exposure changes between indoor and outdoor environments
  • ordinary lens perspective
  • candid reactions and unscripted body language

The camera operator should sometimes walk slightly behind, beside, or in front of the subject instead of maintaining a perfectly composed angle.

Do not make the footage feel like a commercial production, fashion campaign, cinematic trailer, or professionally stabilized travel advertisement.

SUBJECT BEHAVIOR

The subject behaves naturally and does not appear to be acting for the camera.

Include small, believable actions such as:

  • checking a phone
  • adjusting hair or clothing
  • looking around before crossing a street
  • briefly acknowledging the camera
  • laughing at something happening outside the frame
  • becoming distracted by shops, food, people, or the surrounding environment

Avoid continuous eye contact, exaggerated expressions, dramatic posing, or model-like movements.

TIMELINE

00:00–00:05 — Morning departure

The subject leaves a comfortable apartment carrying a small backpack.

They briefly check their phone, adjust their hair, smile naturally toward the camera, and begin walking through a quiet neighborhood.

Soft morning daylight. Local residents are beginning their day. The camera follows from slightly behind with natural walking movement.

00:05–00:12 — Exploring the city

The subject walks through local streets, enters a small independent café, orders a drink, and briefly talks to the camera.

They continue through a street market or neighborhood shopping area, look at small stores, take casual photographs, and react naturally to the surroundings.

The camera remains close but imperfect, occasionally shifting position to avoid pedestrians or objects.

00:12–00:20 — Travelling toward the coast

The subject takes public transportation or walks toward the seaside.

Show the environment gradually changing from dense city streets into a quieter coastal town.

As the ocean becomes visible, the subject reacts with genuine excitement.

The sea breeze moves their hair and clothing naturally. The camera follows them onto the beach as they walk near the water, examine a seashell, and watch the waves.

00:20–00:27 — Afternoon with friends

The subject meets a small group of friends near the beach.

They talk, laugh, walk near the shoreline, and casually play beside the water.

The camera moves naturally between people as though documenting a real shared memory rather than directing a scene.

Include overlapping movement, partially obstructed framing, spontaneous reactions, and brief moments where someone looks toward the camera unexpectedly.

00:27–00:30 — Sunset ending

During golden hour, the subject sits near the ocean holding a cold drink and quietly watches the sunset.

The camera remains still for a moment, then slowly steps backward to reveal more of the shoreline, waves, friends, and evening sky.

End with the emotional feeling of an authentic personal travel memory rather than a polished cinematic conclusion.

VISUAL STYLE

Authentic personal travel vlog footage.

Realistic smartphone or compact mirrorless camera appearance.

Natural daylight and location-based lighting.

Documentary realism.

Subtle digital sensor texture and realistic video compression.

Believable skin texture, fabric movement, wind interaction, reflections, shadows, and environmental motion.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No commercial cinematography.

No excessive cinematic color grading.

No perfectly stabilized gimbal movement.

No dramatic slow motion.

No artificial transitions.

No montage-style visual effects.

No beauty-filter appearance.

No exaggerated acting.

No fashion-model posing.

No constant eye contact with the camera.

No impossible camera movement.

No duplicated people.

No face morphing.

No identity drift.

No hairstyle changes.

No body-proportion changes.

No sudden outfit changes.

No extra fingers or malformed hands.

No frozen background characters.

No text overlays.

No subtitles.

No logos.

No watermarks.

```

拆解：這組 Prompt 好在哪裡

### 1. 它先定義拍攝者，而不是只定義畫面

很多 AI 影片 prompt 只寫「cinematic travel video」。這組提示詞先說清楚：畫面是由「朋友」跟拍，而不是攝影團隊拍攝。這會影響鏡位、穩定度、構圖、主角反應與整體可信度。

### 2. 它要求模型保留拍攝痕跡

手持晃動、偶爾失焦、曝光改變、focus breathing、路人遮擋，這些在傳統廣告攝影裡可能是瑕疵，但在 AI 影片裡反而是去除塑膠感的關鍵。

### 3. 它把主角行為寫得很生活

主角會看手機、整理頭髮、過馬路前看周圍、被店家或食物吸引、對鏡頭短暫反應。這些微行為比「beautiful woman walking gracefully」更有真實感。

### 4. 它把一天拆成連續時間線

從公寓出門、城市探索、前往海邊、下午與朋友互動，到夕陽收尾，整段有空間與時間推進。這比單純列出景點，更能讓模型理解 Vlog 的記憶感。

### 5. 它的負面約束很精準

不是只寫 `no bad quality`，而是明確排除商業攝影、過度穩定、戲劇化慢動作、模特擺拍、持續看鏡頭、臉部漂移、髮型漂移、身形漂移與背景人物凍結。

可改寫模板

如果要換場景，不必整段重寫，只要替換 timeline：

  • 城市版：公寓 → 地鐵 → 咖啡館 → 書店 → 夜市 → 河岸夜景。
  • 校園版：宿舍 → 早餐店 → 教室 → 圖書館 → 社團活動 → 傍晚操場。
  • 美食版：市場 → 小吃攤 → 咖啡館 → 巷弄散步 → 朋友聚餐。
  • 日本旅行版：旅館 → 車站 → 商店街 → 神社 → 海邊電車 → 夕陽。

保留不變的是「朋友跟拍規則」：手持、輕微晃動、自然反應、不完美構圖、不要廣告感。

使用提醒

  • 若使用真人 reference image，需取得本人同意，避免肖像權與 deepfake 風險。
  • 如果要商業用途，需確認人物、場景、音樂、店家招牌與可識別路人是否可使用。
  • 角色一致性仍可能在臉型、髮型、手部、服裝與身形比例上漂移，需要人工審稿。
  • 不要把「反 AI 感」誤解成低品質；目標是可信的生活紀錄感，而不是模糊、髒污或失控。
  • 若平台有 AI 生成標示規則，發布時仍應標註 AI 生成或 AI assisted。
